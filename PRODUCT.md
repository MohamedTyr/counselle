# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Student applicants using Counselle as a focused workspace for college-admissions planning, school tracking, tasks, deadlines, activities, and essays. They are usually in a high-stakes decision workflow where clarity, trust, and fast scanning matter more than decoration.

## Product Purpose

Counselle helps students think through and manage the college-admissions process with an AI agent and structured application workspace. Success means the interface feels calm, precise, and credible while keeping admissions work easy to inspect and act on.

## Brand Personality

Focused, quiet, premium. The product should feel deliberate and intelligent without becoming sterile, generic, or visually loud.

## Anti-references

LibreChat, generic SaaS dashboards, bright playful student-app colors, glassy gradient AI interfaces, noisy card grids, and decorative design patterns that distract from real admissions work.

## Design Principles

- Keep the workspace calm enough for repeated daily use.
- Make important admissions state scannable without shouting.
- Treat visual polish as system-level craft, not one-off styling.
- Preserve trust through clarity, contrast, and restrained motion.
- Let the sidebar establish the product identity and extend that language across the app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone for status. Maintain readable foreground colors across dark surfaces and muted UI states.
