# College Essay Essentials → Counselle skills — structure proposal

Status: proposed, awaiting owner decision. Raw book extracts: `plans/essay-skill/extracts/1-5*.md`.
Method: 5 Sonnet readers extracted the full book (258pp) with fidelity; Opus synthesis pass designed the structure; final judgment by main agent.

## Verdict: multiple skills — 3 public doors + 8 internal library skills

- A monolith is impossible publicly (the book ≈ 70k chars of load-bearing content vs. the 12,000-char public cap) and wrong internally (an agent reading feelings word-banks to fix an ending writes a worse ending). The book's knowledge is extremely moment-specific.
- The split axis is **moment of need**, not book chapter: no topic → topic-but-no-draft → has-a-draft, plus repairs reached by diagnosis.
- `load_skill` is the zero-code progressive-disclosure mechanism: every internal skill automatically appears in every agent's load_skill menu (verified in app/skills.py — internal bodies have NO size cap; menu filters only on selection_group). No hub/router skill: the menu IS the routing table, so every description is written as a routing predicate ("load when…"), internal ones one short clause each.

## The skill set

### Public (composer picker)
1. **`essay-brainstorm`** — "Find my topic" (~6k chars). Essence Objects as interview (20 objects, verbatim permission lines, worked examples), objective-correlative theory, Core Values 10→5→3→1 funnel, deepest-story gut test, mini follow-ups, traps (agent proposing material for the student; first-safe-answer; topic ≠ deep). Hands off to essay-drafting via the two type questions.
2. **`essay-drafting`** — "Draft an essay" (~6.5k). Two-question type picker verbatim → A/B/C/D; the permissions (don't have to write challenges/career); narrative-for-A/C, montage-for-B/D as tendency; draft-within-24h momentum rule; "just start your darn essay"; proportional word budgets as guides; one-night emergency path (with the book's own warning); the word_count==0 direct-write exception (from panel plan); personal-statement-vs-supplement border → why-us/community supplements hand off to existing `essay-fit`.
3. **`essay-revision`** — "Review and revise" (~8.5k, the largest public). The essay's job + four qualities; **the Great College Essay Test verbatim** (the diagnostic engine); triage table mapping test failure → repair skill; the five-step first-sentence revision verbatim; CUT/REWRITE/ADD word-limit method; suggestion mechanics from the panel plan (small edits, batch independence); Four Qualities of an Amazing Essay; risk nuance (great writing ≠ safe college essay; non-traumatic topics pass the Test just as well); agent-as-feedback-giver rules ("what I've seen work" never "what colleges want"; "It's your essay. You get final say."); when-to-scrap.

### Internal (load_skill library; descriptions = one trigger clause)
4. **`essay-honesty`** (always applied; per panel plan C1 stays non-invokable). Fabrication steals the content, over-polishing steals the voice — one principle, two halves. The propose/assert line: agent may OFFER menus (value bank, Big List) and PROPOSE candidates, never ASSERT into prose unconfirmed — this is what turns every book worksheet into a legal interview. Worked examples; placeholder marking; voice protection (fragment vs. error, register is content, ask before regularizing); crisis boundary (disclosure of active crisis → stop editing, respond as a person; the "still in it" gate is a topic rule not a wellbeing rule).
5. **`essay-values`** (~4k). The full ~100-value bank verbatim (embedded once, referenced everywhere); predictable-values rule verbatim + paired examples + the real rule (the more usual the value, the better the "so what" must be); the guess test as agent procedure; amazing-[career] qualities; value density ("Santur": 5 values in 148 words); CUT/REWRITE/ADD; "your values ARE your dreams and aspirations."
6. **`essay-structure`** (~4.5k). Exactly two structures; six narrative beats verbatim (+ Dark Night of the Soul; escalate stakes more than once; new status quo must differ); order not fixed; montage + focusing lens (3 tips verbatim; NO soundtrack/mixtape essays; lens doubles as thesis); checkmark/valley arc + two-dip variant; blending (worked cases); first-sentence structural principle.
7. **`essay-exercises`** (~7k). The stalled-student fallbacks: **the Big List of ~60 brainstorm questions verbatim** (highest-value verbatim asset — a model doesn't spontaneously ask "When did you know?"), 21 Details, Everything-I-Want-Colleges-to-Know (+ routing items to statement/activities/additional-info/supplement), Time Line (+ agent-adapted partner variation), unblocking moves.
8. **`essay-types`** (~10k, largest — deliberate exception to the 120-line convention; verbatim banks sit below a divider). All four step-by-step guides at full fidelity: Type A 5-column worksheet as interview (negative-effects-only rule + why, threads across columns, aim for 2–3); Type B write-backward 3-column (center first, ≥5 values ≥2 unusual, ask-a-professional questions verbatim, show 2/3 → reveal → tell 1/3, "so what?" repeated); Type C victor-not-victim (word-budget trick, 3-part structure max-1/2 challenge, the two pitfalls + fixes verbatim, paint-by-number outline with all sentence starters, smallest→largest, stand-up-and-cheer moment); Type D (patience/8–10 drafts, six framing devices with worked cases, every-career-is-a-placeholder philosophy); the ~50-item challenges checklist near-verbatim + both permissions; **the challenge-topic safety gate verbatim** ("Are you still in it, or have you come through it?"; metabolized-challenge REMEMBER box; "you don't need to shock the reader"); red flags → Additional Information.
9. **`essay-depth`** (~8k). Feelings and Needs Exercise in full as 6-column interview + both word banks verbatim (26 feeling categories, 10 need categories) + template sentences + go-one-level-deeper; Want vs. Need (outside/inside story, in-opposition depth, worked catalog, the two tips: externalize the lesson; wrong-way→realization→right-way pattern); four vulnerability methods (judgeable reveal; unresolved/unresolvable contradiction — writing-to-report vs. writing-to-discover, conflicting values as the essence of drama; unapologetic passion; unresolved ending + Hollywood-ending rejection prompt); insight-distance test (predictable tell → rewrite; no tell possible → cut the image); make-the-reader-cry.
10. **`essay-craft`** (~6.5k). Openings (problem-that-must-be-solved, don't solve till the end; senseless-image variation + both keys; quiet openings are fine); sounding smart honestly (guardrail first — "a really fine pig"; great questions unanswered; geeky language + contextualizing phrase; show-then-tell + painter/critic/curator + "be both"; insight = something you see the reader doesn't, 3–5 per essay); brag-via-value (value in the paragraph's first sentence); endings baseline ("surprising, but inevitable", Venn sweet spot, last-line callback pattern, so-what fallback); style points (four so-what questions verbatim; big words selectively; complex thoughts succinctly).
11. **`essay-advanced`** (~6k). Gate: pass the Test first; match difficulty to writer. The five techniques with ratings: Setup/Payoff 5.2 (Chekhov both halves; cut hanging guns; the layered-setup map); Verisimilitude 4.4 (Flaubert; precision not symbolism; saves Hallmark endings); Dark→Beautiful 9.1 (choose details, find the universal; NEVER present darker-as-strategy; repeat one-line safety gate); Sixth Sense Ending 7.5 (McKee mechanism; memorable-but-buried hints; misdirection; three-level ladder; **the don't-preplan rule binds the AGENT**: find the connection latent in the student's own paragraphs, never propose the reveal up front); Make It New 8–10 (supplements only, framing note). Don't over-explain endings.

## Routing
No hub. Three doors from the picker; agent chains via load_skill (each body opens "load essay-honesty first" and closes with named handoffs). Resolution examples: "no idea what to write" → brainstorm; "is my draft good?" → revision → triage → one repair; "feels like a résumé" → depth; "ending sucks / mention my award" → craft; "make it stand out" → revision-confirm → advanced; "should I write about my depression?" → types (safety gate); "why Northwestern" → existing essay-fit. Typical conversation cost ≈ 18–24k chars of skill text.

## Cut (value×ease)
Author bio/testimonials; partner/accountability templates (the agent IS the partner; keep "also get one human reader"); book navigation; web/resource plugs; personality-assessment generator (one clause); encouragement prose (keep "you aren't falling behind" — it's a coaching move). The 24 essays compress to one-line structural exemplars for ~11 recurring ones. **The coaching frameworks themselves are NOT compressed** — every exercise, worksheet column, technique, difficulty rating, trap, sentence-starter, and all word banks survive at or near verbatim.

## Key resolved tensions
- Worksheets → interviews under the propose/assert line (fabrication-safe).
- Draft-in-a-day vs. 8–10 drafts: speed is for the first draft, patience for everything after.
- Word banks and Big List: embed verbatim (cheap; the direct antidote to "perseverance, dedication, hard work").
- Four types vs. mid-draft arrivals: taxonomy is a drafting tool, not a gate; revision never asks "what type."
- Dark-material policy deliberately duplicated (types = full gate; advanced = one-line repeat at the second decision point) + new crisis boundary in honesty.
- Don't-preplan-the-twist binds the agent itself.

## Divergences from the essay-ai-panel reserved skill set (plans/essay-ai-panel.md §8, C1)

- D2: **`essay-voice` dropped as public**, folded into `essay-honesty` (voice protection is an always-on rule, not a chooseable mode — same argument C1 uses for honesty). OPEN PRODUCT CALL: keep a thin public "Sharpen my voice" anyway if the picker entry has product value.

- D4: `essay-revision` broadens to diagnosis-then-execution (the Test + triage) — prescription without diagnosis is the feature's failure mode. Fallback if too big: split public essay-review / essay-revision.
- D5: panel-plan mechanical rules preserved verbatim, relocated (batch independence → revision; word_count==0 exception → drafting).
- D6: the 8-skill internal library is new; zero code (drop directories in skills/).
